About This Product

Give your skin a fresh, radiant-looking glow with our Vitamin C Glow Serum. This lightweight formula is designed to fit easily into your everyday skincare routine.

Use it consistently as part of your morning routine and follow with moisturizer and sunscreen.

Key Ingredients

Vitamin C Helps brighten the appearance of skin.
Hyaluronic Acid Helps maintain a hydrated skin feel.
Vitamin E Helps condition and soften the skin.

How To Use

  1. Cleanse your face and pat dry.
  2. Apply a few drops of serum to your face.
  3. Gently massage until absorbed.
  4. Follow with moisturizer.
  5. During the day, finish with sunscreen.
